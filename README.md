# Peak Paint OS

Custom browser-based business operations system built for a trade contractor.

## What it does

Peak Paint OS brings core office and field workflows into one application, including:

- Quoting and tendering
- Rates and pricing libraries
- Project scheduling
- Health & safety workflows
- Variations
- Timesheets
- Invoicing and reporting
- Management and field/mobile views
- Controlled job release to field staff
- Site H&S sign-off before work starts
- Tender export workflows
- Reusable company settings

## Project approach

The system was designed around real operational workflows rather than generic software features. The objective was to replace fragmented manual processes and disconnected tools with one practical interface that follows a job from initial setup and pricing through delivery, field use and reporting.

## Role

Business systems design, workflow mapping, UI/UX, application development, testing and iterative improvement.
